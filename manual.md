## Brief Highlights

* **Fully-digital, multi-theme, open-innovation hackathon**: There are no fixed problem statements, only themes. Projects must fall in at least one of those themes.
* **Optional Sponsored Tracks**: Fetch additional points and make you eligible for additional prize money.
* **Team Size**: 1-4 members. Team formation is a necessary step even for solo participation.
* **Multiple Projects**: Each team can submit up to 3 projects. Prizes are considered at the project-level (a team can win multiple prizes for multiple projects).
* **No Shortlisting/Filtering Rounds**: All teams are expected to build and submit their final projects.
* **Virtual Demo Day**: The Top 25 projects will be invited to participate in a one-day long virtual demo day to present their projects to a panel of experts.

## Themes

HACKHAZARDS ‘26 is a software-first hackathon. Participants are encouraged to choose any problem statement, build for any user group, use any tech stack/framework, integrate AI/Web3, and ship in any form (web app, mobile app, desktop app, browser extension, API, or platform).

Your project must align with at least one of the following 10 themes:

1. **Human Experience & Productivity**: Software that enhances how individuals live, think, and work. (Productivity tools, habit-building systems, mental health platforms, accessibility solutions, personal AI assistants, etc.)
2. **Climate & Sustainability Systems**: Software addressing environmental challenges and enabling sustainable practices. (Climate analytics, carbon tracking, energy systems, agri-tech, waste management, environmental monitoring, etc.)
3. **HealthTech & Bio Platforms**: Software solutions focused on healthcare, wellness, and biological systems. (Telemedicine, health data platforms, diagnostics, wearable integrations, preventive care, medical/bio research tools.)
4. **Learning & Knowledge Systems**: Software that enables learning, education, and knowledge sharing. (EdTech platforms, AI tutors, peer-to-peer learning, skill development tools, knowledge management/research tools.)
5. **Work, Finance & Digital Economy**: Software powering how individuals and organizations create and exchange value. (Fintech, SaaS, creator economy tools, marketplaces, remote work tools, new economic models.)
6. **Infrastructure, Mobility & Smart Systems**: Software that operates and optimizes large-scale systems. (Logistics, mobility solutions, smart city systems, IoT dashboards, campus infrastructure, real-time system monitoring.)
7. **Trust, Identity & Security**: Software that ensures safety, privacy, and trust in digital systems. (Authentication, identity management, cybersecurity tools, fraud detection, privacy-preserving technologies.)
8. **Media, Social & Interactive Platforms**: Software that enables communication, content creation, and digital interaction. (Social platforms, community tools, gaming, content distribution, interactive/immersive experiences.)
9. **Public Systems, Governance and Civic Tech**: Software designed for governments, institutions, and societal systems. (Public service platforms, governance tools, civic engagement, nonprofit tools, crisis response/resource coordination.)
10. **Developer Tools & Software Infrastructure**: Software that enables developers to build and scale other software. (APIs, SDKs, backend systems, cloud infrastructure, deployment tools, databases, developer productivity platforms.)

## Sponsored Tracks and Challenges

Participating in these tracks is optional. They unlock additional partner-specific prizes and bonus points.

### 01. Expo Track - Build Mobile Apps With Expo
* **About the Partner**: Expo is a platform built on React Native that simplifies mobile app development, testing, and deployment.
* **Objective**: Build a mobile-first application designed for mobile (not just web adapted to mobile screens).
* **Mandatory Tech**: Expo (React Native). Must provide a working demo (APK, Expo Go link, etc.).
* **Prizes**: USD 500 (1st: 250, 2nd: 150, 3rd: 100) cash to the top 3 teams.
* **Special Points**: 20 points.

### 02. Neo4j Track - Build Databases with AuraDB
* **About the Partner**: Neo4j is a graph database platform. AuraDB is its fully managed cloud service.
* **Objective**: Relationships must be central to the product backend architecture and powered by AuraDB.
* **Mandatory Tech**: Neo4j AuraDB as primary database with real queries executed.
* **Prizes**: USD 500 (1st: 250, 2nd: 150, 3rd: 100) cash to the top 3 teams.
* **Special Points**: 20 points.

### 03. Base44 Track - Build Prototypes with Base44
* **About the Partner**: Base44 is a rapid prototyping platform.
* **Objective**: Build a functional prototype/MVP showing a complete working user flow (no static screens).
* **Mandatory Tech**: Base44 must be central to the finished prototype.
* **Prizes**: Partner-specific details.
* **Special Points**: 10 points.

### 04. Sarvam Track - Build AI Applications with Sarvam
* **About the Partner**: Sarvam builds multilingual and voice AI foundational models/infrastructure.
* **Objective**: Integrate AI where Sarvam APIs play a central role.
* **Mandatory Tech**: Sarvam APIs (must show live AI interactions/workflows).
* **Credits**: 1000 credits per team (Claim via the [Hackathon Dashboard](https://www.namespace.world/hackathon-dashboard/29fe1a24-ef95-4a24-bba2-388b4b2cc89f)).
* **Prizes**: Sarvam credits worth USD 1,000 (1st: 500, 2nd: 300, 3rd: 200) to the top 3 teams.
* **Special Points**: None.

### 05. Render Workflows Track - Build Durable AI Agents & Automated Systems
* **About the Partner**: Render Workflows is a durable execution and orchestration engine for background jobs, AI agents, pipelines, and workflows.
* **Objective**: Automate complex multi-stage tasks executed reliably in the background.
* **Mandatory Tech**: Render Workflows with at least one multi-stage workflow, deployed live on Render (submit logs/execution flow evidence).
* **Credits**: $50 USD in Render credits per participant (Claim via [GitHub Portal](https://credits-portal-mmdm.onrender.com/claim/hackhazards)). More info at [Render Credits Docs](https://render.com/docs/credits).
* **Prizes**: Render credits worth USD 900 (1st: 500, 2nd: 300, 3rd: 100) to the top 3 teams.
* **Special Points**: None.

## Project/Build Submission Guidelines

All projects must be submitted on the NAMESPACE Platform. The submission must include:
1. **GitHub Repository Link (Mandatory)**: Must contain a README following the template in this [demo repository](https://github.com/pradeeptosarkar/hackhazards26-demo-repository).
2. **Demo Video Link (Mandatory)**: Public video, strictly under 5 minutes, showing working project functionality.
3. **Deployment Link (Highly Recommended)**: Evaluators should be able to run/use the project, or download the APK/executable.
4. **PPT Link (Highly Recommended)**: Presentation limited to a maximum of 6 slides.
5. **Team Verification**: Ensure all team members are showing up correctly before submitting. No changes can be made after submission.
